Scenester gathers live music, comedy, film, theater, dance nights and museum shows from venue calendars, and shows you what's happening tonight, from the big halls to the back rooms.

For venues

Run a room, club, theater, cinema or gallery in one of our cities? Add your venue and we'll list your public shows for free, with links straight to your own tickets.

How it works

From the source

Shows come from venues' own calendars and ticketing pages, refreshed several times a day.

Yours

Import artists from Apple Music or Spotify, follow venues, and get alerts when your artists announce a show.

Tickets stay with you

We link to each venue's own tickets. Scenester doesn't sell tickets or take a cut.
